Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

MADKING IQ SRL ("we", "our", or "us") provides ARROW UDA, a mobile puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use our game.

2. Account and Gameplay

ARROW UDA can be played without creating an account or signing in. The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, including unlocked levels, completed stages, gameplay statistics, Daily Quest completion dates, and gameplay preferences.

This information helps maintain your game progress and Daily Quest calendar records. ARROW UDA does not currently offer account-based cloud progress synchronization.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

ARROW UDA uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may include banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and advertising-related information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Information .

4. Notifications

ARROW UDA may offer optional local gameplay reminders. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay preferences and progress may be stored on your device. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

In ARROW UDA, open My Zone > Privacy Rights to review privacy information, access the Privacy Policy, and delete locally saved gameplay progress.

Deleting local progress resets saved stage progress, gameplay statistics, and Daily Quest completion records. Sound, vibration, and reminder preferences may remain saved.

Open My Zone > Privacy Preferences to review available advertising privacy options. Where supported and applicable, Google consent-management options may be available. Notification permissions can also be managed in your Android device settings.

Local gameplay information generally remains on your device until you delete it through the game, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Deleting local gameplay data does not automatically delete information independently processed by third-party advertising providers.

For additional information, see our Data Deletion Instructions. You may also contact us with privacy-related requests.

7. Children's Privacy

ARROW UDA is intended for a general audience, including children under the age of 13. The game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

We configure Google Mobile Ads requests with child-directed treatment and a G-rated maximum ad content rating for all users. Google and its advertising partners may process certain device and advertising-related information as described in Section 3.

Parents or guardians can review the privacy choices and local data deletion options described in Section 6. For questions or concerns about a child's privacy, please contact madkingiqsrl@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our game, services, or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
