ARROW UDA — Data Deletion Request

Last updated: November 5, 2026

ARROW UDA is a mobile puzzle game developed by MADKING IQ SRL. We respect your privacy and provide a way to request deletion of applicable personal data under our control.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

ARROW UDA does not require an account or login. To submit a data deletion request:

  1. Send an email to madkingiqsrl@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: ARROW UDA — Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe the data you want deleted and provide information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant data.
  4. We will review your request and respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws.
Request Data Deletion

2. Local Gameplay Data

Game progress, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored gameplay data by clearing ARROW UDA's app storage through Android settings or uninstalling the game. Clearing app storage may reset your progress.

3. Advertising Data

ARROW UDA uses Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and related information. Such information is subject to the relevant third-party privacy policies and retention practices.

For Google's privacy controls, visit Google Data & Privacy .

4. Data Deleted and Data Retained

We will review requests to delete applicable personal data held under our control. ARROW UDA does not maintain user accounts. Local gameplay data is generally controlled on the user's device.

Some information may be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, or legitimate compliance purposes. Third-party advertising data is governed by the respective providers' policies and retention periods.
